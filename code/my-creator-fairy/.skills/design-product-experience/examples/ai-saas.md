# Example: AI SaaS Introduction Deck

## Product Truth

A research assistant imports notes, groups evidence by topic, drafts a cited brief, and requires approval before export.

## Story Arc

1. Cover: product name and cited-brief category
2. Research fragmentation problems
3. Before and after artifact
4. Mechanism reveal: notes -> topics -> draft -> citations -> approval
5. Human-control boundary
6. Three real analyst scenarios
7. Evidence and missing proof
8. Start a brief CTA

## Motion

Reveal the mechanism manually while preserving prior stages. Use a fade for the approval-boundary slide. Show the complete pipeline for reduced motion and PDF.

## Visual Direction

Editorial workbench, neutral canvas, evidence teal, approval green, warning amber, and serif headings only for chapter titles.
