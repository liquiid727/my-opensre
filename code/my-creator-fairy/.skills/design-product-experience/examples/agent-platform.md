# Example: Agent Platform Introduction Deck

## Product Truth

The platform creates tasks, reviews plans, approves tool permissions, shows execution traces and artifacts, retries failed steps, and hands work to humans.

## Story Arc

1. Cover: controllable agent operations
2. Problems with opaque autonomous execution
3. Product definition and unit of work
4. Lifecycle overview
5. Plan review reveal
6. Permission-boundary walkthrough
7. Execution trace walkthrough
8. Failure, retry, and human handoff
9. Artifact and audit trail
10. Real operating scenarios
11. Team rollout stages
12. Open workspace or request-access CTA

## Primary Visuals

Use product screenshots or simplified walkthrough frames of the queue, plan, permission dialog, trace, and artifacts. These are evidence inside the introduction, not a generated dashboard implementation.

## Motion

Reveal lifecycle stages in order. Keep task identity and status fixed while the active stage changes. Reduced motion shows the complete lifecycle.
